Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data Cravio collects and how we use it. The data controller is Mayank Shrivastava, trading as Cravio, of 57 Whitefriars Drive, Harrow Weald, Harrow HA3 5HW, United Kingdom ("Cravio", "we"). Contact: infoatkhoj@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, phone number (optional) and password (stored only in encrypted, hashed form).
  • Location: the postcode you enter and its approximate coordinates, used to show meals near you. On your profile we only show the first part of your postcode (for example "SW1A"), never your full address.
  • Orders: what you ordered, when, the amount paid and the order status.
  • Messages and reviews you send through the app.
  • Photos you upload (meal photos, profile picture). We remove embedded location data from uploaded photos.
  • Device data: a notification token so we can send order updates.
  • Cooks: approval and food-safety information you provide. Identity and bank details for payouts are collected and held by Stripe, not by us.

We do not see or store your full card number. Card payments are handled by Stripe.

Why we use it (and our legal basis)

  • To run your account, process orders and payments, and pay cooks: performance of our contract with you.
  • To keep the platform safe, prevent fraud and handle complaints: legitimate interests.
  • To keep payment and tax records: legal obligation.
  • To send push notifications: your consent, which you can withdraw in your phone's settings.

Who we share it with

  • The other side of your order: a cook sees the customer's first name and order; a customer sees the cook's first name and collection details.
  • Stripe (payments and cook payouts).
  • Google Firebase (push notifications).
  • Our cloud hosting provider, which runs our servers and database on our behalf.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it (for example a food safety investigation).

We don't sell your personal data or use it for third-party advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data while your account is open. If you delete your account, we erase your personal details straight away. Records of orders and payments are kept in anonymised form for up to 6 years, as UK tax law requires.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, or object to how we use it. You can delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete account). For anything else, email us. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

We'll post any changes here and tell you in the app if they're significant.
