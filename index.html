How Cravio works

1

Find a meal

Browse dishes listed by approved home cooks near you. Ingredients and allergens are shown on every meal.

2

Pay in the app

Pay securely by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. Card payments are handled by Stripe; we never see your card number.

3

Collect it

Pick up from the cook during their collection window and show your 4-digit pickup code.
