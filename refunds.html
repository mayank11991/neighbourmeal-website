Cancellations & refunds

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Every Cravio order is paid in the app and collected in person from the home cook. This page explains when you can cancel and how refunds work.

Cancelling as a customer

You can cancel an order yourself in the app (Orders → Cancel) until the cook has finished preparing it, while its status is Order placed, Accepted or Preparing.

If you cancel in that window, you get a full refund, including the service fee, to the card or wallet you paid with. You don't need to contact us.

Once the food is marked Prepared or Ready for pickup, the order can no longer be cancelled in the app, because the cook has already made it. If something has gone wrong, contact us (see below).

If the cook cancels

If a cook cancels your order for any reason, you get a full refund automatically.

If a meal sells out while you pay

Occasionally the last portion is bought by someone else while you're on the payment page. If that happens your order isn't placed and the payment is refunded in full automatically.

Problems with your food

If your order was wrong, missing items, or you have a food safety concern, report it in the app or email us within 48 hours of the collection time, with your order number and, where possible, a photo. We review each case and, where the problem is confirmed, refund the order in full or in part.

If you think you have become ill from food bought on Cravio, seek medical advice first, then contact us so we can investigate and, where appropriate, inform the local authority.

Orders not collected

Each meal has a collection window shown before you pay. If you don't collect within that window and haven't cancelled, the order isn't normally refunded, because the food was prepared for you.

How long refunds take

Refunds are sent immediately from our side. Your bank or card provider usually shows them within 5–10 working days.

Contact

Email infoatkhoj@gmail.com with your order number. We aim to reply within 2 working days.
